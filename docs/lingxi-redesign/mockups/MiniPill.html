Chapter 1 · Swindler

The rain had not stopped for three days, and the lantern over the archive door swung on its chain like a slow clock.

Wen Lu climbed the last step, shook the water from his sleeves, and counted the keys on his ring twice. One was missing. It was always the same one.

Inside, the shelves smelled of cedar and lamp oil. Somewhere near the back a page turned, though no one should have been there at this hour.

He set his lantern on the reading table and waited.
